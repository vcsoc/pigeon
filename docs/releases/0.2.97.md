# Pigeon 0.2.97

- Arrows have draggable tail and tip points instead of a bounding-box resize/rotation handle.
- Rectangles and squares have four draggable corner points. Dragging a corner keeps the opposite corner fixed, including rotated rectangles and dragging across the opposite corner.
- No dashed selection outline around arrows or rectangles. Drag the shape body to move it; rotation remains adjustable in the layer panel.
- Newly drawn arrows/rectangles switch to Select for immediate manipulation. Point drags remain grouped as one Undo/Redo edit.

Validation includes both arrow endpoints, all rectangle corners, reversed/rotated geometry, corner crossing, native pointer drags and Undo/Redo.
