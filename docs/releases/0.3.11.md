# Pigeon 0.3.11 — correct thumbnail geometry on first scan

- Newly scanned images now use their decoded width and height as soon as a thumbnail is ready. In justified view, the card's row-width basis and preview aspect ratio update together, without requiring Rebuild Thumbnails or a folder round trip. Virtualized layout continues to refresh its placement while preserving scroll position.
- Existing thumbnail caches, original files, editing behavior, and user preferences are unchanged.

Validation includes a native, isolated first-scan regression with square, portrait, and wide images: the initial card geometry now matches the geometry after leaving and returning to the folder. All 692 unit tests pass, along with packaged first-scan geometry and thumbnail-throughput checks. The separate 1-second new-file-rescan timing target was exceeded on this machine in two packaged runs (1,106 and 1,071 ms); the installed previous version also exceeded it (1,048 ms). A source run passed at 765 ms. This timing target is not claimed as met in the packaged build.

Release-content review: tracked images and video fixtures were inspected for people, readable private details, and embedded metadata; tracked text and available Git history were scanned for common credential patterns and private paths. No private user media or credentials were identified in the publishable project content. An ignored local `.pi-build` image depicting a copyrighted movie poster is **not** tracked or packaged and its reuse rights have not been verified. Branding provenance and legal rights cannot be established by visual inspection alone. macOS distribution remains excluded unless signing and verification succeed.
